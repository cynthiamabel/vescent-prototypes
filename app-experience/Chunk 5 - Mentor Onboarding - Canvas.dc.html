1eDecisions to settle with the team + open questions from Lauren's doc
Calls I made (change if wrong)
Full assessment, not a short one. Mentors take the same 20-Q + lightning + 4H so the match compares like-for-like and gets their 4-letter type.
Bio is collected in onboarding (1c), so bio cards are ready the instant a teen is matched — no chasing mentors later.
Experiences reframed, not dropped — a mentor's "I've been there" is a real match signal.
Gear lives on the mentor home only — role-gated entry to the admin portal, exactly like the current app. Teens never see it.
Open — needs the team / Eric
